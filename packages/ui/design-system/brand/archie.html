Archie

The eight poses, the kit that switches on them, and the size below which the torch becomes a dot.

01 — The eight poses

One drawing, eight jobs

M3 head: the jaw is merged into the head outline, so the head is one closed curve with a nose dot near the bottom. The light is a torch in a front paw, part of #base in every pose — not head gear. Source: packages/ui/brand/archie/.

Once per screen. Two Archies are two states and one of them is lying — the only exception is agentworth.dev/archie, where he is the subject. Never on the landing hero, never in the receipt, never on --json.

02 — The kit

He arrives bare

The accessory is data-accessory on the SVG root; the colourway is an .archie-cN class on any ancestor. Neither is a second drawing and neither is a second file to keep in sync.

C3 and C4 follow the app's theme. C1 and C2 are deliberate, fixed choices — they do not flip with the toggle above, and that is on purpose.

03 — The size rule

Under 40px the torch becomes the lit dot

At 24px one SVG unit is half a pixel: the torch body is 2.2px tall with 0.8px of ink on each side, and what survives is a smear where the beam was. So the root takes data-size="small" — the torch hides, a lit disc with a soft halo shows at the paw, and the nose grows so it cannot read as an open mouth.

packages/ui/Archie.tsx sets the attribute from its size prop (ARCHIE_SMALL_BELOW = 40). Anything rendering the files directly — a favicon build, a static rasteriser — sets it itself.

04 — The terminal form

Nine columns, three lines

,---. ( o o ) -* '._.' archie ──────────····· 68%

The torch glyph at the paw is the state: * on, o sweeping, - nothing, . off. The beam dash sits in front of it, so "nothing" prints -- and "off" prints -. — accepted, in exchange for the torch being attached to a paw rather than floating. The one-line form stays (*) archie.
