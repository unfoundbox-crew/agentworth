Motion rules

Durations, easing, entrances vs. exits, reduced motion, and AgentWorth's two signature entrances — the receipt print and the scan.

01 — Durations

Five named durations, no invented values

TokenValueUse
--motion-fast120msHover, color, press
--motion-base160msChips, rows, small entrances
--motion-slow220msCards, popovers
--motion-slower300msDialogs, sheets — the ceiling, not a default
--motion-exit120msEvery exit, on every surface

--motion-exit exists as its own name rather than an alias of --motion-fast so a call site can't accidentally make an exit as slow as its entrance.

02 — Easing

Two curves cover almost everything

TokenCurveUse
--ease-outcubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)Default — entering or exiting
--ease-in-outcubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1)Moving or morphing in place

Never ease-in on UI — it starts slow, holding back the exact frame the user is watching for.

03 — Entrances vs. exits

Entrances travel. Exits don't.

Entering is the system explaining itself: fade plus a 10px rise, at --motion-slow. Leaving is the system getting out of the way: fade only, in place, at --motion-exit — faster, and it never travels anywhere.

04 — Reduced motion

Zeroes distance, keeps time

The media query only strips --mv-rise, --motion-enter-scale, and --stagger-step to nothing — it never shortens a duration. A viewer who asked for reduced motion still sees a fade; they just don't see anything travel or scale while it happens.

Check it, then replay "Play enter" — the card still fades in, it just doesn't rise or scale.

05 — Receipt printing

Lines arrive top to bottom, staggered

The signature AgentWorth entrance: a receipt's line items appear in sequence rather than all at once, echoing a thermal printer. Each line fades and rises at --motion-base, staggered ~90ms apart — capped well under the 40ms-times-six ceiling the studio's stagger rule sets, because a receipt reads top-to-bottom and can afford a beat.

06 — Scan progress

A determinate bar, never an indeterminate spinner

Progress that has a known end fills left to right with transform: scaleX()/width on a linear clock — never eased, because a scan's rate is the truth being communicated. Reserve loop tokens (spin/pulse/shimmer) for genuinely indeterminate waits.
