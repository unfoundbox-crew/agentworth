ARCHIE — TERMINAL SHORT FORM
Drawn at 100 columns, monospace, exactly as it prints. Glyph set: ASCII, the
box-drawing and block ranges U+2500-259F, and exactly five more — ● ○ · — →.
That is the CLI's whole allowed set (docs/DESIGN.md, apps/cli/src/ui/mod.rs); a
tick and a cross are outside it, because Geist Mono and SF Mono are both
missing them and a missing glyph falls back to another face at a different
advance width, shifting every column to its right. No emoji, ever, for the
same reason: half the terminals people run agents in render one at the wrong
width and shear the box open.

Archie survives the reduction to three things: the drop ears (the parentheses),
the merged jaw (the third line), and the torch (the glyph at the paw). Only the
torch glyph changes between states. Everything else is fixed, because a figure
that redraws every frame reads as noise in a scrolling log.


THE FIGURE                                                   9 columns × 3 lines

       ,---.        the crown of the head — no strap, no head gear
      ( o o )       the ears, hanging past the eyes
     -*  '._.'      the torch in his front paw, and the merged jaw


THE TORCH GLYPH IS THE STATE

     *    on         idle, and the moment he finds something
     o    sweeping   digging — the light is moving
     -    dim        searched here, nothing
     .    off        error

     No colour is required for any of this. It reads over ssh, in a pipe, and
     in a CI log with the colour stripped.

     One cost, named because it is real: the beam dash sits in front of the
     glyph, so "nothing" prints -- and "off" prints -. rather than a single
     distinct character in a fixed column. That is the price of attaching the
     torch to a paw instead of floating it beside him, and it was paid on
     purpose.

THE SCAN LINE                                            one line, any width ≥ 48

 (*) archie  scanning  ──────────────────·······  68%  1,204 sessions

     The one-line form is the ears and the light: ( * ). At three characters
     the parentheses have stopped being ears — they are a frame around a state,
     the same way [ok] is — and nobody reads a headlamp into it. It is the only
     form that fits a status bar, a spinner slot, or the right edge of a
     prompt, so it keeps its shape and the columns get spent on the 3-line
     figure instead.

 (*) on      ( o ) sweeping      (-) nothing      ( ) error


THE DIG LOOP                                     4 text frames · 1400ms · 350ms a frame

   frame 1        frame 2        frame 3        frame 4

     ,---.          ,---.          ,---.          ,---.
    ( o o )        ( o o )        ( o o )        ( o o )
   -*  '._.'      -o  '._.'      -*  '._.'      -o  '._.'
    ──────  .      ─────· ·       ────·  ·       ─────  ·

     The ground line frays and the chips walk right, then reset. In a terminal
     that cannot repaint in place — a piped log, a CI transcript — print frame
     1 once and nothing after it. A loop that scrolls is a loop that lies.


THE FOUND BEAT                                        plays once, 220ms, then holds

   digging                found                  handed over

     ,---.                  ,---.                  ,---.        ,--.
    ( o o )                ( O O )                ( o o )       |==|
   -o  '._.'              -*  '._.'              -*  '._.'--    |= |
    ────·  ·                 ────                               |##|
                                                                '~~'

     Three steps: the light stops sweeping and holds, the eyes widen, the
     receipt comes up. The receipt is the brand mark — the two neutral line
     items and the one violet total. In the terminal the total is the ### row,
     and it is the only row drawn heavy.


THE ERROR BEAT                                    120ms, no travel, then it stops

     ,---.          the torch goes out first
    ( - - )         then the ears drop and the eyes flatten
   -.  '._.'

     Nothing slides, nothing bounces, nothing spins afterwards. The failure
     line prints under it and the figure holds still:

 ( ) archie  ○ no session index at ~/.claude/projects — run `agentworth scan`


WHAT NEVER APPEARS IN THE TERMINAL

     · Archie in the receipt itself. The receipt is evidence; a mascot on it
       makes it look like marketing.
     · Archie on `--json`, `--quiet`, or any machine-readable output.
     · A spinner made out of Archie. He is a state, not a progress indicator.
     · The headlamp. It is an optional costume on the SVG and has no terminal
       form at all — in the terminal the light is always in his paw.
